WEEK 06 FIELD LOG DIGITAL FABRICATION & PROTOTYPING FORGE INNOVATION & VENTURES

Laser Cutting & 3D Printing (Digital Fabrication & Prototyping)

Converting 2D/3D digital designs into physical prototypes using precision subtractive laser cutting (RDWorks V8) and additive manufacturing (Bambu Studio + Bambu Lab H2S).

Part 1: Subtractive Fabrication — Laser Cutting
Step 01 — Laser Cutting Fundamentals

I explored the basic working principles of laser cutting:

Power & Speed: Balancing laser energy and cutting speed for clean edges.
Focus & Kerf: Setting correct focal distance and accounting for material removed.
Vector & Raster: Vector paths for cutting and raster scanning for engraving.
Air Assist: Removing debris, reducing flare-ups, and keeping lens clean.
Step 02 — RDWorks V8 Workflow

I prepared a DXF vector design of a crest badge and processed it in RDWorks V8.

Workflow: Design → DXF → Import → Position → Layer Settings → Preview → Cut
Fig 1. RDWorks V8 toolpath configuration, raster engraving layers & vector simulation.
Step 03 — Acrylic Fabrication
